# Coding notes: SEC AI interview, Participant 11 (Spanish-language)

- Source file: transcripts/AI Interviews/SEC/Participant 11 SP/Participant 11 SP.docx
- Site: Southeast Community Pantry (SEC)
- Type: AI-agent interview (interviewer "Alex"), conducted in Spanish; transcript is the English translation. Conducted while the client sat in her car with her children, having just picked up her food.
- Language: Spanish
- Format / locator scheme: docx, `Name • HH:MM:SS`. Quotes are translated, not verbatim.
- Participants present: 1 client (with children in the car), 1 AI interviewer
- Read in full: yes

## 1. Values

- Non-discrimination and being seen as part of the community, not a case.
  - "they do not discriminate against you, they see you as another family united with the community." (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:13:44; translated)
- Help arriving at the right moment, and staying.
  - "they helped me at the precise moment I needed it. They have always been there helping" (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:15:27; translated)
- Kindness and patience of the English teacher.
  - "the teacher was very kind, very patient" (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:15:12; translated)

## 2. Capabilities and Constraints

- Capability: drives, uses multiple services (English classes, financial
  counseling), and is embedded in the school's parent community.
  - "I attended English classes, I also had financial counseling at the beginning of the year, and they have helped me a lot" (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:14:14; translated)
- Capability: efficient trip-chaining.
  - "I go, pick up my children from school, and at the same time, I pick up my food." (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:13:20; translated)
- Constraint: children in the car, limited parking, and a hard time limit
  because she has to drive home.
  - "I have my children here in the car, they are making a lot of noise" (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:15:48; translated)
  - "I can't finish it because I am going to drive now." (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:20:15; translated)

## 3. Meanings and Practices

- Practice of mobility: one trip does school pickup and food pickup together.
  The pantry works because it fits inside a trip she already makes.
- Meaning: the pantry is where she also learns English and manages money, and
  where the parent book-reading meetings happen. It is a community hub, not a
  food stop.

## 4. Misalignment

- Tension: the AI interview was run on a parent sitting in a car with noisy
  children, blocking a parking spot, needing to leave. The AI repeated "what
  motivated you to continue" four times (15:14:47, 15:14:57, 15:15:04,
  15:15:26), did not register her stated constraints, and pushed to extend
  the interview when she was trying to go.
  - Value at stake: relational trust, and respect for the client's time and
    context.
  - "I am in an interview, children, please understand, no? And I can't finish it because I am going to drive now." (Participant 11 SP.docx, Interviewee 15:20:15; translated)
  - the AI's response: "Would you like to extend the interview by about 2 more minutes" (Participant 11 SP.docx, Alex 15:19:53; translated)
- On the pantry service itself: no misalignment. She is "very satisfied with
  everything" and asks for it to stay as it is.

## 5. Provocation

- Speculative prompt present: no.
- No provocation material. The Spanish-language AI handled the language but
  not the context.

## Cross-bucket notes

- Strong evidence that SEC functions as a community hub for Spanish-speaking
  parents: school meetings, English classes, financial counseling, and food
  all in one place, reached on the school-pickup trip. This is close to the
  "Mobility as Social Service" ideal in practice, minus the AV.
- The AI-interview failure here is not linguistic (it spoke Spanish) but
  situational: it could not tell that the person was mid-errand with children
  and needed to leave. A recurring shape across the AI interviews.
