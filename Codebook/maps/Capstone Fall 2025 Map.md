# Capstone Fall 2025: AV x Food Pantry Ideation Map

Source: `Capstone_Fall_2025.pdf`. One giant whiteboard page (about 35,255 x 8,052 pt), FigJam-style.
Topic: how autonomous vehicles (AVs) can support food pantries, food banks, volunteers and the neighbors they serve.
Every note is a card or sticky placed inside or near a hand-drawn circle. **The circle is the cluster; the text label next to it is the cluster name.**

---

## 1. How to read this file

**Structure.** Clusters (C01..C18) are numbered in board order, left to right. Each cluster has:
- **Label**: exact text on the board, including tags like `(effect)`.
- **Layout**: where it sits and any oddities.
- **Statements**: ideas, claims, scenarios (mostly white cards, plus some colored stickies).
- **HMW**: "How might we..." questions (mostly colored stickies).

**Placement flags.**
- `[rim]` = note straddles the circle edge. Assigned to the nearest cluster.
- `[outside]` = note sits just outside the circle but clearly belongs with it.

**Text handling.**
- Note text is verbatim, with extraction glitches fixed (split words, missing spaces).
- Original typos are kept, with the likely intent in `[brackets]`.
- Sticky author stamps (all read "Crystal Habib") are omitted.
- Sticky colors are not encoded.
- `[icon]` = an icon or glyph that did not extract.

**Label tags on the board** show what kind of thing each cluster is:

| Tag | Meaning | Clusters |
|---|---|---|
| `(features)` | What the AV is or does | C02 Modular AV, C05 Communication |
| `(effect)` | Outcome or benefit of using AVs | C03, C08, C10, C12, C14 |
| `(challenge)` | Problem to address | C06 Food Desert |
| `(touch point)` | Where AV meets people or process | C15 Food Logistics, C16 delivery tree |
| no tag | Theme, actor or need | all others |

**Cross-cluster links drawn on the board (arrows).**
1. C15 circle ("cause and effect") -> **Role of AV (Storage)** and -> **Role of Pantries (Social Hub)**.
2. C16 caption ("AV's + AI Robot + Human Touch point at delivery") -> **Distributing (Storaging)**, -> **Logistical Carrying and Physical**, -> **Logistical Coordination / Social Connection**.
3. C16 "(Last Mile, touchpoint)" -> long curved arrow -> **Seniors** (C17), under the label "Accessibility? (for disabled people)". The label **"Neighbors Friends"** sits under this arrow and leads into the people-focused clusters C17 and C18.
