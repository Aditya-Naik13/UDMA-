## 1. Values

- Carrying a family alone after loss, with a shrinking support network.
  - "Their mom got killed in a car crash six years ago, and I've been raising them by myself after my husband died four years ago of cancer" (Participant 01.docx, turn 6, Interviewee)
  - "As much family anymore as I used to." (Participant 01.docx, turn 15, Interviewee, on what she can lean on)
- Skepticism that speaking up leads anywhere.
  - "I don't know if somebody's going to actually listen or whatever" (Participant 01.docx, turn 21, Interviewee)

## 2. Capabilities and Constraints

- Capability: full-time kinship caregiver and the family's driver. She
  arranges her own errands around the grandchildren's schedule and now also
  drives the older grandchildren.
  - "I have to come pick up the kids anyways from the Boys and Girls Club, so it's easy location." (Participant 01.docx, turn 8, Interviewee)
  - "right now I have to play Uber and Lyft to my older grandkids that are having transportation issues." (Participant 01.docx, turn 18, Interviewee)
- Capability/constraint pair: wants part-time work but her caregiving and
  driving obligations set the limit.
  - "I am not looking for a full time job. I was wanting to do a part time job but right now I have to play Uber and Lyft" (Participant 01.docx, turn 18, Interviewee)
- Constraint: benefit cliff. The household did not qualify for food stamps.
  - "we didn't qualify for food stamps, so help with food." (Participant 01.docx, turn 5, Interviewee)
- Constraint: registering with the pantry was itself hard. She frames this
  visit as the first time she got through.
  - "This is actually the first time I've gotten through to register" (Participant 01.docx, turn 8, Interviewee)

## 3. Meanings and Practices

- Practice of mobility: trip-chaining. The pantry works because it is on the
  route she already drives for the Boys and Girls Club pickup.
- Practice: she is the transportation of last resort for the extended family,
  driving grandchildren who otherwise cannot get where they need to go.
- Meaning: mobility is caregiving. Her trips are almost all on someone else's
  behalf.

## 4. Misalignment

- Tension: getting registered was a repeated barrier. She names it three
  times as the thing that finally worked "this time."
  - Value at stake: equitable access.
  - "This is actually the first time I've gotten through to register" (turn 8);
    "It was easy this time, as the first time that I had been here and was able to get in." (Participant 01.docx, turn 19, Interviewee)
- Tension: a referral that went nowhere. She submitted information for
  counseling and never got a response.
  - Value at stake: relational trust.
  - "I had took in information and I never heard back from them." (Participant 01.docx, turn 13, Interviewee)
- Tension: the AI interview leaves her unsure it matters.
  - Value at stake: relational trust.
  - "I don't know if somebody's going to actually listen or whatever, but it was okay doing it." (Participant 01.docx, turn 21, Interviewee)
