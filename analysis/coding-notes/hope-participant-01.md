## 4. Misalignment

- Tension: the AI interview format itself. The interviewer loops a scripted
  line at least four times, cuts the client off mid-sentence, and does not
  engage the client's real relational bids (the Medicare flyer, offers to
  help others).
  - Value at stake: relational trust. A client offering to give is processed
    only as an interview subject.
  - Alex repeats verbatim across 09:08:31, 09:08:45, 09:09:18, 09:09:30:
    "There are no right or wrong answers here. By sharing your thoughts, opinions, and experiences, you'll help us understand what elements of the experience could be improved."
  - the client's relational offer goes unacknowledged except by "Understood" (Participant 01 Hope.docx, Alex 09:09:04)
- Otherwise: the client reports no barriers to asking for help and no unmet
  request. "No clear misalignment surfaced" on the pantry service itself in
  this transcript; the misalignment is in the research instrument.
