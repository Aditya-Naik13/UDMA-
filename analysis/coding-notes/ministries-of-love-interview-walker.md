## 1. Values

- The pantry as reciprocal community-building, not one-way charity.
  - "it's a good way of getting back to the community, especially during like these troubling times" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 00:36, Speaker 2)
  - "knowing that they make an impact in my life and I make an impact in their life, and so, like, one of those things where it's like, I have to keep going." (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 03:42, Speaker 2)
- Stability, not abundance, as the shared aspiration.
  - "a lot of people. They don't really want the lavish lifestyle really, like they just want stability. They want to be able to. Have what they need, have it, and then be able to get what they want." (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 03:18, Speaker 2)
- Faith and being rooted for by others as what sustains.
  - "the spirit of Jesus. And then also just like the people that have. Rooted for being wanted me to continue" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 03:42, Speaker 2)
- The pantry as inspiration: seeing it makes him want to do the same when he
  is more stable.
  - "It inspires me a lot to do something like that, when I'm in a more stable place." (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 10:01, Speaker 2)
- Judges the pantry as serving well: right foods, right portions, reaching
  the people who need it.
  - "they're serving the right foods and the right portions. They're making sure everyone's able to give food" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 02:20, Speaker 2)

## 2. Capabilities and Constraints

- Capability: he is the household's cook and food-getter for a two-person
  home; his sister works long hours, he also works.
  - "I'm the one that cooks for the household, and so making sure people like to get food to make for the house and ingredients" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 01:15, Speaker 2)
- Capability: physical capacity and routine. He walks everywhere as a matter
  of course.
  - "I walk here. It's not too far a walk probably like 30 minutes, which that's not too far for me" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 01:46, Speaker 2)
  - "I walk everywhere, so" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 08:15, Speaker 2)
- Capability / aspiration: pursuing pro-wrestling training, already
  volunteering at the school, accepted to start in February or March.
  - "they already are willing to accept me with open arms" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 05:45, Speaker 2)
- Capability/constraint pair: he can walk to the pantry easily, but cannot
  carry a loaded return trip comfortably.
  - "the most challenging part is going back because I have the bags of the food and whatnot, so getting it home" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 01:46, Speaker 2)
- Constraint: store geography. The affordable stores are a two-hour walk; the
  only close store is expensive.
  - "the closest all these around here. It's like a two-hour walk for me." (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 08:15, Speaker 2)
  - "the only other grocery store around here is Kroger, and it's pretty expensive, and so it's like, I don't go there often" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 08:30, Speaker 2)
- Constraint: food prices.
  - "the prices of everything going up in the world, um, grocery stores seeing, like, astronomical highs" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 07:29, Speaker 2)
- Constraint: wants a more stable and higher income, though current income
  covers the bills.

## 3. Meanings and Practices

- Meaning of mobility: walking is his default and his identity ("I walk
  everywhere"). It is not framed as deprivation, but the load is the limit.
- Practice: walks 30 minutes each way to the pantry carrying bags home; walks
  or occasionally goes with someone to Aldi or Costco; buys in bulk.
  - "sometimes, I'll be able to have someone to go with them to a grocery store and get the food that I need" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 08:30, Speaker 2)
- Practice he has ruled out: the bus, specifically because bulk shopping and
  bus travel do not mix.
  - "I usually tend to buy bulk, and so like carrying all that onto the bus... it would it messed with my nerves a bit" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 09:05, Speaker 2)
- What moves: groceries and cooked meals for a household of two, on foot.

## 4. Misalignment

- Tension: the food environment. The affordable grocery options are a
  two-hour walk from a carless household, and the reachable store is priced
  out of routine use. The pantry is filling a gap created by store siting and
  pricing.
  - Value at stake: equitable access.
  - "the closest all these around here. It's like a two-hour walk for me." (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 08:15, Speaker 2)
- Tension: public transit does not fit the grocery trip. A bulk shop cannot
  reasonably be carried on the bus, so the bus is not a real option for the
  main food run.
  - Value at stake: equitable access, and flexibility for variable need (the
    system serves a commuter trip, not a provisioning trip).
  - "carrying all that onto the bus... it messed with my nerves a bit" (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 09:05, Speaker 2)
- Small tension: the pantry does not stock drinking water, which is heavy and
  costly to obtain for people without a car.
  - Value at stake: flexibility for variable need.
  - "It would be cool if they had, like. A case of water... because a lot of people really need water. That's a big thing." (Quincey AI shashi.docx, 06:42, Speaker 2)

## 5. Provocation

- Speculative prompt present: no. No AV or future-mobility prompt was asked.
- No provocation material in this transcript.
- Coder note for the synthesis: this participant is close to the exact profile
  an AV service would target (carless, walks everywhere, load-limited, food
  desert, two-hour walk to affordable groceries) and yet no speculative prompt
  was put to him. That is a gap in the interview design, not a finding.

## Cross-bucket notes

- The load, not the distance, is the mobility problem. He can walk 30 minutes
  or 2 hours; what he cannot do is carry a bulk shop or a case of water home.
  Any mobility design implication should target the return-with-goods leg.
- "they just want stability" is a strong, quotable articulation of the
  variable-need theme from the community side, phrased as a general claim
  about what people want from systems.
- A rare fully coherent interview in the AI-interview set, because it was
  conducted by a human who let it be a conversation.
