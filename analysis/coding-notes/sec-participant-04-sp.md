## 1. Values

- Kindness, organization, and the pantry as a place of interaction and
  meetings, not just distribution.
  - "Very good, they hold meetings, good interactions, they help people." (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:10:52; translated)
  - "the people who assist us are very kind and everything is well organized." (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:11:33; translated)
- A modest, concrete aspiration: a quiet place of one's own.
  - "everyone dreams of a quiet place, their house, their car, well, a better living situation." (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:13:05; translated)

## 2. Capabilities and Constraints

- Capability: working, and getting by.
  - "thank God, I am working, but not every day, but something comes up." (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:13:58; translated)
- Capability: has a car and is paying it off.
- Capability / aspiration: wants to learn to read and speak English and use
  the pantry's education benefits.
  - "Learn how to read a bit of English and study a bit and take advantage of the benefits offered by this place" (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:13:30; translated)
- Constraint: irregular work income.
- Constraint: car payment is sometimes hard to make.
  - "currently I owe a car and sometimes the payment is difficult" (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:14:15; translated)
- Constraint: language, and only knowing about wraparound help by hearsay.
  - "I haven't received that, but they help a lot, they say, with people who have difficulty paying for housing" (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:12:14; translated)

## 3. Meanings and Practices

- Practice of mobility: drives; the pantry is close and the trip is simple.
- Meaning: thin. The car is both an asset (mobility) and a monthly strain
  (payment), which is itself a small finding: for this client, being mobile is
  a debt.

## 4. Misalignment

- Tension: a translator is provided but it is not enough. The client wants
  Spanish-fluent assistance and English teaching, i.e. an ongoing relationship
  in their language, not an interpreter at the counter.
  - Value at stake: relational trust, and equitable access.
  - "They always have a translator, but it would be good to have someone who can assist us in Spanish a bit and teach us to speak a bit of English." (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:14:37; translated)
- Tension: wraparound services (housing help, financial aid, medical) are
  known only as rumor to this Spanish-speaking client and have not reached
  them.
  - Value at stake: equitable access.

## 5. Provocation

- Speculative prompt present: no.
- Adjacent, automation reaction (positive): "Good, good, first time, but very
  good." (Participant 04 SP.docx, Interviewee 16:15:21; translated). The
  Spanish-language AI interview was a positive first experience.

## Cross-bucket notes
