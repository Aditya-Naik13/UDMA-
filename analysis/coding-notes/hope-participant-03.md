    "I don't know." (Participant 03 Hope.docx, Alex 08:44:53, Interviewee 08:44:59)
- Tension: uncertainty about the legitimacy of asking, tied to the

  - Value at stake: relational trust.

- On the instrument: the client disengages, answering "Skip", "I don't know",

## Cross-bucket notes

- This transcript is the clearest small case for a design implication about
  the first visit: the friction is not the food, it is not knowing whether
  you belong, whether you qualify, or whether taking help is fair to others.
  An automated intake that only extracts answers makes this worse.
- "Income predictability" as the thing the client most wants to improve is a
  precise articulation of the flexibility-for-variable-need theme from the
  client side.
